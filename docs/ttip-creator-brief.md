### Ada, your assistant
Ada lives inside Ttip and answers 24/7. She can see your account, so she gives you real answers about *your* money — not a help article.

She can also do things for you:

- **"Send ₦5,000 to 9077984753 Opay"** — she checks the account name with the bank and shows you who you're paying before you approve it.

- **"Buy me ₦500 airtime"** — straight to your line.

**Ada never moves money on her own.** She gets it ready, shows you exactly what will happen, and you approve it with your PIN.

## Tone

**Do:** direct, warm, Nigerian. Plain English. Short sentences. Real amounts — "₦5,000", not "funds". Show the app rather than describe it.

**Don't:**

- ❌ **Promise returns or profit.** Ttip is for moving money, not investing. Never imply crypto will go up.
- ❌ **Say "zero fees" or "no charges."** It isn't true, and it's the opposite of what makes Ttip good.
- ❌ **Guarantee timing.** "Usually seconds" is true. "Always instant" isn't.
- ❌ **Show a real account number, BVN, or anyone's balance** on screen.
- ❌ **Call Ttip a bank.** It isn't one.
