## 1. The one-liner

**Ttip turns crypto into African cash in seconds — and lets you send it to anyone, even people who aren't on Ttip yet.**

Longer version, for a caption:

> Deposit any coin on any chain. Get paid in naira, cedis, shillings or rand at a sharp rate. Cash out straight to your bank — or tip anyone with a link.

## 2. Who it's for

- **Nigerians and Africans paid in crypto** — freelancers, remote workers, traders, creators — who need it as spendable local money without a sketchy P2P trade.
- **Anyone tired of P2P.** No haggling with a stranger, no waiting for release, no scam risk, no locked account.
- **People who send money often** — market traders, families, small businesses.

Primary market is **Nigeria**. Ghana, Kenya and South Africa are live for bank payouts too.

## 3. The core loop — three steps

1. **Deposit any crypto.** BTC, ETH, USDT, USDC, SOL and more, across **70+ chains**, to your own address. Or transfer naira straight from your bank.
2. **It becomes local money.** Converted at a live, sharp rate with the spread shown up front.
3. **Cash out or tip.** Straight to your bank account in seconds, or tip anyone with a shareable link.

## 4. Features

### Money in
- **Any coin, any chain** — 70+ networks, one address per network. Non-custodial deposits: they're settled through a provider, not held by us.
- **Your own naira account number.** Verify your BVN and Ttip issues you a dedicated account. Transfer to it from any bank app and it lands in your balance in seconds.

### Money out
- **Bank payouts in seconds** to Nigerian banks — and Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, Uganda, Tanzania and Rwanda.
- **Crypto withdrawals** to any external wallet.
- **Multi-wallet auto-debit.** Sending ₦50,000 but it's spread across your USDT, naira and rand? Ttip works out what to take from where. No manual swapping first.

### Tipping — the bit that's actually different
- **Tip anyone by @username**, instantly and free between Ttip users.
- **Tip people who aren't on Ttip yet.** Share a link; the money waits for them. This is the feature nobody else in the market has.

### Spending
- **Airtime and data** on MTN, Glo, Airtel and 9mobile.
- **Electricity, TV/cable and internet** bills.

### Ada — the AI assistant
This is the headline feature and the most demo-able thing in the app.

Ada is built into Ttip and answers 24/7. She can **see your actual account** — your balance, your limits, why a transfer is pending — not just read from a FAQ.

She can also **do things**:
- **"Send ₦5,000 to 9077984753 Opay"** — she confirms the account name *with the bank* and shows you who you're paying before you enter your PIN.
- **Photograph an account and send it to her.** Standing in a market, the vendor's details on a board or a phone screen — snap it, add "send 7k to this", done. No typing ten digits in a hurry.
- **Talk to her instead of typing.** Tap the mic and say it.
- **"Buy me ₦500 airtime"** — straight to your line.
- **Ada on Telegram.** Connect your account in one tap and do all of it from a Telegram chat. Your PIN is deleted from the chat the second she reads it.

**Ada never moves money on her own.** She *prepares* it, shows you exactly what it will do, and you confirm with your transaction PIN.

### Earning
- **Refer & Earn — 25% of your friends' fees, for life.** Not a one-off bonus: an ongoing cut.
- **First-deposit bonus** — a referred friend gets ₦500 after their first $10+ deposit has stayed on the platform 72 hours.
- **Cashback** — 0.15% back on activity, claimable from ₦5,000.
- **Streaks and a leaderboard.**

### The app itself
- **Works everywhere** — install it to your home screen on iPhone or Android, or use it in a browser.
- **Light and dark mode.**
- **App lock and a transaction PIN.**
- **Receipts as images or PDFs**, ready to share.

## 9. Tone

**Do:** direct, warm, Nigerian. Plain English. Short sentences. Real amounts (₦5,000, not "funds"). Show the app rather than describe it.

**Don't:**
- ❌ Promise investment returns, profit, or that crypto will go up. Ttip is a **payments** app, not an investment.
- ❌ Say "zero fees" or "no charges" — it isn't true and it's the opposite of the brand.
- ❌ Guarantee timings. "Usually seconds" is true; "always instant" isn't.
- ❌ Show a real account number, BVN, or anyone's balance on screen.
- ❌ Call it a bank. Ttip isn't a bank.
